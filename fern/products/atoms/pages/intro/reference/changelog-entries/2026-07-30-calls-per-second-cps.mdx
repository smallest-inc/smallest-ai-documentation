---
title: Calls per second (CPS) — pace outbound dialing to your carrier's limit
---

Outbound calls are now paced by a **calls-per-second** limit alongside concurrency. Instead of throttling requests on your side to protect your telephony trunk, submit in bulk and declare the rate your carrier accepts: the queue releases calls at exactly that rate and nothing is dropped.

For **imported (SIP) numbers**, org admins set the rate per **SIP trunk** on the Concurrency page — every number on a trunk shares one rate, because that's where carriers enforce it. New imports start at the safe floor of 1 call/sec, and changes bind within about a second, including for calls already queued. **Rented** Smallest numbers are paced per provider by your plan.

New endpoints: `GET /product/cps-limits` and `PATCH /product/custom-trunk/cps-limit`.
