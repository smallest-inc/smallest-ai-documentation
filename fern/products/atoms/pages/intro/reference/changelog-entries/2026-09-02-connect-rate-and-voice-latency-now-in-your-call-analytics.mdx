You can now see how many of your outbound calls actually connect, not just how many you attempted. Two new analytics endpoints report attempts vs. connections with a connect rate over time, and perceived conversation latency broken down by pipeline stage (speech-to-text, LLM, text-to-speech). The dashboard summary also gains connected calls, connect rate, cost per connected call, and average/P95 latency, so the top-line numbers answer "how many are connecting, how responsive are they, and what does each connection cost?"

```
GET /api/v1/analytics/connect-rate-timeseries?dateFrom=2026-08-24T00:00:00Z&dateTo=2026-08-31T23:59:59Z&granularity=day
GET /api/v1/analytics/latency-summary?dateFrom=2026-08-24T00:00:00Z&dateTo=2026-08-31T23:59:59Z
```
