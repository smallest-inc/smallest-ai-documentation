## What your agent will be able to do

Once connected, Calendly will expose the same scheduling tools as [Cal.com](/voice-agents/integrations/scheduling/cal-com):

| Tool | What it does |
|------|---------------|
| **Check availability** | Looks up open slots on your connected event type |
| **Book meeting** | Creates a new booking for a caller |
| **Cancel meeting** | Cancels an existing booking |

As with Cal.com, these are granted per agent — you won't have to enable all three.

Native Calendly scheduling isn't available yet, but you don't have to wait to give your agent calendar access:
