[Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/) is Google's scheduling and calendar management tool. Connect it to a Smallest AI agent so callers can check availability, book, reschedule, or cancel meetings, and your agent can look up what's coming up next — all on your primary calendar, without leaving the call.

---

## What your agent can do

| Tool | What it does |
|------|---------------|
| **Check availability** | Looks up open slots on your primary calendar |
| **Book meeting** | Creates a new event for a caller |
| **Reschedule meeting** | Moves an existing event to a new time |
| **Cancel meeting** | Cancels an existing event |
| **List upcoming events** | Looks up what's already on the calendar |

These are granted per agent from the Tools tab after you connect — you don't have to enable all five. Google Calendar is the only scheduling integration with a "what's on my calendar" tool.

---

## Connecting Google Calendar

Unlike Cal.com and Calendly, Google Calendar connects via **OAuth** — no API key or token to generate or paste.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    Find Google Calendar on the Integrations page and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with Google">
    You'll be redirected to Google's sign-in screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant permissions">
    Authorize Smallest AI to view and manage events on your calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the connection">
    You should see "Connected" status with your Google account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Using Google Calendar in your agent's prompt

```
CALENDAR TOOLS (Google Calendar):
Your agent can check availability, book, reschedule, or cancel
meetings, and look up what's already scheduled.

When the caller wants to schedule:
1. Ask what they'd like to book.
2. Ask for their preferred date.
3. Check availability and offer 2-3 time slots.
4. Confirm their selection before booking.
5. Book the meeting and confirm the details out loud.
```

```
Caller: "Can you move my meeting to Thursday afternoon?"

Agent: "Sure — let me check what's on the calendar first."

Agent calls: list_events
Response: existing meeting found on Tuesday at 2:00 PM

Agent: "I see your meeting Tuesday at 2 PM. What time Thursday works?"

Caller: "3 PM."

Agent calls: reschedule_meeting
Response: reschedule confirmed

Agent: "Done — you're now set for Thursday at 3 PM instead. Anything
        else I can help with?"
```

## Good to know

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Operates on your primary calendar" icon="calendar">
    Google Calendar tools check, book, reschedule, cancel, and list events on the connected account's **primary calendar** — not secondary or shared calendars.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Full reschedule support" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    Unlike Calendly, Google Calendar has a real reschedule endpoint, so moving an existing meeting is a single action — no need to cancel and rebook.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Connection failed" icon="xmark">
    **Possible causes:**
    - Google sign-in was cancelled or denied
    - OAuth token expired or was revoked

    **Solutions:**
    1. Reconnect the integration and complete the Google sign-in flow
    2. Check that access hasn't been revoked under your Google Account's third-party access settings
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No availability showing" icon="calendar-xmark">
    **Possible causes:**
    - No open slots on the primary calendar in the requested range
    - Wrong Google account connected

    **Solutions:**
    1. Check availability directly in Google Calendar
    2. Reconnect with the correct Google account
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Event not created or updated" icon="plus">
    **Possible causes:**
    - Permissions were only partially granted
    - API error

    **Solutions:**
    1. Reconnect Google Calendar and re-grant permissions
    2. Check logs for the specific error
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
