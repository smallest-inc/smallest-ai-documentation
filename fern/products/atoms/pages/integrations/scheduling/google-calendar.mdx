## Connecting (planned)

Google Calendar will connect via **OAuth** — sign in with your Google account and grant Smallest AI access, the same pattern used for Salesforce and HubSpot today. No API key to generate or paste.

## What your agent will be able to do

Once connected, Google Calendar will expose the same scheduling tools as [Cal.com](/voice-agents/integrations/scheduling/cal-com) and [Calendly](/voice-agents/integrations/scheduling/calendly):

| Tool | What it does |
|------|---------------|
| **Check availability** | Looks up open slots on your connected calendar |
| **Book meeting** | Creates a new event for a caller |
| **Cancel meeting** | Cancels an existing event |

As with Cal.com and Calendly, these will be granted per agent — you won't have to enable all three.
