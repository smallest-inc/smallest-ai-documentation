title: "Language lists: reconcile Lightning v3.1 Pro + Pulse pages against the live API"
---

Two long-standing documentation gaps corrected against live-API probes.

**Lightning v3.1 Pro — Dutch and Swedish now on the voices/languages page.** The Pro model card already listed `nl` and `sv` as accepted codes with voice-count pending. The [Voices and Languages](/models/documentation/text-to-speech-lightning/voices-languages) page was missing both from the European accordion and the summary table. Both codes synthesise successfully today via any multilingual Pro voice (verified end-to-end against `POST /waves/v1/tts`).

**Pulse pre-recorded — the correct number is 26 languages, not 22.** The [Pulse model card](/models/model-cards/speech-to-text/pulse) documented 22 batch codes in five places, but the batch spec enum has been 26 for a while. Latvian (`lv`), Estonian (`et`), Danish (`da`), and Maltese (`mt`) were missing from the batch language table. Total across streaming + batch (with 12-code overlap) is now `35 documented languages` throughout.

Language counts on the Pulse Pro card and the Multi-Asian aggregator descriptions were already consistent with the spec; only the standard Pulse card was stale.
