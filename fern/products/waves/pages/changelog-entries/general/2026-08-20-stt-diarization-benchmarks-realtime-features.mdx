title: "STT: diarization benchmarks + VAD, endpointing, and detection features on the realtime overview"
---

Two documentation updates on the Pulse STT surface.

**Diarization page: performance table.** The [Speaker diarization](/models/documentation/speech-to-text-pulse/features/diarization) page now includes a Performance section with internal-benchmark DER (Diarization Error Rate) numbers for both batch and streaming modes, and end-to-end round-trip RTF against Deepgram, ElevenLabs, AssemblyAI, and pyannoteAI. Numbers are scored with `pyannote.metrics` (no collar, overlapping speech counted).

**Realtime WebSocket features overview: five cards added.** The [Realtime features overview](/models/documentation/speech-to-text-pulse/realtime-web-socket/features) now surfaces VAD Events, Endpointing, Age & Gender Detection, and Emotion Detection alongside the existing feature cards. All four already had dedicated feature pages; they were missing from the overview.
