---
title: "Speech to Text: VAD events on the live WebSocket API reference"
---

The Pulse STT live WebSocket now models the acoustic voice-activity events in the API reference: `speech_started` and `speech_ended`, emitted alongside `transcription` messages when the connection sets `vad_events=true`. See [VAD events](/models/speech-to-text/features/vad-events) for the payloads and usage.

The events have been in production; this documents them on the WebSocket reference.
