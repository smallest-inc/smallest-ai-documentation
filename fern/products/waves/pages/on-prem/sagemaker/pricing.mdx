Your bill has two components:

1. **Software.** Smallest AI's per-hour rate per running instance, charged via AWS Marketplace.
2. **Infrastructure.** AWS's per-hour SageMaker Real-time inference rate, based on instance type.

Both land on your normal AWS invoice.

## Software rate

Per-hour, per running instance. Bills for wall-clock time the endpoint stays `InService`, not per request.

## Infrastructure rate

AWS sets these rates, publishes them on the [SageMaker pricing page](https://aws.amazon.com/sagemaker/pricing/), and varies them by region.

Reference rates in `us-east-1` (verify on the AWS page before quoting):

| Instance | On-demand rate |
|---|---|
| `ml.g5.xlarge` | ~$1.41/hour |
| `ml.g5.2xlarge` | ~$1.69/hour |
| `ml.g5.4xlarge` | ~$2.72/hour |

**Savings Plans** and **Reserved Instances** apply the same way as any other SageMaker workload; a 1-year no-upfront commitment typically knocks 30 to 40 percent off the on-demand rate.

## Private offers

For volume, longer commit terms, or non-standard EULA edits, contact `support@smallest.ai` with:

- Target monthly instance-hours
- Preferred commit length (1 or 3 years)
- AWS account IDs to grant the private offer to

Private offers land in the [AWS Marketplace Private Offers](https://console.aws.amazon.com/marketplace/home?#/private-offers) console. Accept there and Terraform or the console handle the rest transparently.

## Estimating a month

```
monthly_cost = (software_rate + instance_rate) × 24 × 30 × instance_count
```

Worked example, one always-on endpoint at 1 instance on `ml.g5.xlarge` in `us-east-1`:

- Software: _placeholder rate_ × 720 hours
- Infrastructure: $1.41 × 720 = **$1,015.20/month**
- **Total:** filled in once the software rate is captured from the listing

Autoscaling changes `instance_count` over time, so the accurate way to model a variable workload is to look at CloudWatch `Invocations` over a representative window, convert to instance-hours, and multiply.

## Version upgrades

Each pinned software version has its own published rate. Upgrading in place (create a new endpoint config pointing at the new version's Product ARN, then update the endpoint) may change the software rate. Marketplace displays the new rate before you commit.

## What you are NOT billed for

- **Marketplace subscription itself.** Zero cost when no endpoint is running.
- **`sagemaker:InvokeEndpoint` calls.** Counted against the software plus instance rate above, not billed per call.
- **CloudWatch metrics at default granularity.** Custom metrics or detailed monitoring bill separately per AWS's normal CloudWatch rates.
