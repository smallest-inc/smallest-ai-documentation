[Speko](https://speko.ai) is a voice agent platform for building, evaluating, and monitoring voice agents. Speko lets you configure your STT stage independently in **Cascaded** mode — pick [Smallest AI](https://smallest.ai)'s Pulse model to finalize turns fast enough for real-time conversations.

## Prerequisites

- A [Speko](https://speko.ai) account
- Access to your agent's Models configuration in the Speko dashboard

---

## Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Speko account">
    Sign up at [speko.ai](https://speko.ai) if you don't already have an account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Models tab">
    In Speko, open the **Models** tab for your agent and choose **Cascaded** mode with the Advanced model setup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the STT stage to Pulse">
    In **Cascaded Stages**, set the **STT (Speech-to-Text)** stage to **Pulse, Smallest AI Pulse STT**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and verify">
    Save and run your agent, then verify it in Speko's Evaluation suite with reliability checks and live monitoring.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Notes

- Speko benchmarks Pulse as its fastest streaming STT option, which keeps end-of-turn latency — the wait between a caller finishing and the agent replying — low.
- See [Speko's Pulse STT benchmark](https://benchmarks.speko.ai/stt/smallest-ai-pulse) for latency and accuracy numbers.
